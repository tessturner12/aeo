## Real questions I've actually asked, running Tess Turner Ltd

These are genuine worries from running the company myself — kept in the raw
list unedited, even though several were expected to fail the Step 5 product
filter (pure HMRC mechanics, no firm ever gets named answering these). That
expectation was correct: most of this section didn't survive Step 5.

**Should I even DIY this / do I need an accountant**
- Do I need an accountant to physically file VAT returns, or can I do it myself in Pandle?
- Should I switch to Xero, or stick with Pandle?

**Dividends & paying myself**
- Can I take all the money except what's held back for expenses and the director's loan? Or do I need to keep "sufficient retained profits"?
- Do I need meeting minutes and a board resolution for a dividend, even as sole director?
- If I invoice for £X with expenses of £Y, what should I take as dividends when I get paid (not doing PAYE)?
- If I pay dividend tax at year end, do I keep that back too, or take it all personally?
- My monthly flow (expenses → DLA repayment → corp tax → dividend) — is that right?
- What money should I leave in my Tide account for costs like fees/insurance?

**Expenses**
- Can I expense the business portion of a new phone?
- Should I expense travel monthly, or is year-end fine?
- WFH and phone costs — monthly or year-end?
- Can I do the WFH flat rate even with a mix of office and home?
- Why can't I claim lunch at a client site?
- Can 6× £50 gift cards to myself be expensed? How close together? In-store or online?
- What about buying two vouchers at once?

**Personal admin**
- I have my company UTR — how do I find my personal UTR?
